## Rules

- Every page below the top level carries breadcrumbs (current page a non-link) matched by BreadcrumbList schema.
- Every commercial page exposes the consultation CTA in both header and closing section.
- Filtered Work/Insights views MUST canonicalise to their base index and MUST NOT become indexable thin pages.
- 404 is `noindex` and excluded from the sitemap.
- No route depends on a query parameter to function; the Map Your Stack handoff parameter is optional and allowlist-parsed.
