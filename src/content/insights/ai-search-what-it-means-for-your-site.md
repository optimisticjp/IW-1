## Two different AI stories, kept separate

There are two AI opportunities, and confusing them causes most of the noise. The first
is public discovery: customers now find businesses through search results, maps, video,
marketplaces and AI-assisted answers. The second is AI working inside the business, on
enquiries, support and repeated work. This article is about the first.

## What actually helps you show up

Being ready for AI-assisted answers looks a lot like being ready for search in general.
It rests on strong technical foundations, clear and useful content, accurate business
information and suitable structured data. There is no separate trick.

Importantly, **no agency can guarantee that an AI system will recommend a brand**. Anyone
who promises that is overselling. The right goal is to make the business easier to
discover, understand, trust and cite, and then to keep the information accurate.

## Why we do not sell it as a separate service

Because it is not one. AI-search readiness is a section of good search work, not a
standalone product with its own promise. Treat it as part of your foundations, and you
are ready for however discovery keeps changing.
