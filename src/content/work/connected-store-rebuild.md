---
title: A connected store rebuild for a growing retailer
capabilityTags: ['build', 'convert', 'connect']
audienceTags: ['ecommerce-brands']
situation: A retailer had a store that looked fine but sat apart from their advertising, email and reporting, so nothing learned from anything else.
obstacle: Sales landed in the store, marketing ran on guesswork, and every tool reported a different number, so no one could agree on what was working.
customerExperience: Shoppers who left without buying kept getting the same generic messages, and returning customers were treated like strangers.
change: We rebuilt the store on a platform that could connect, wired in tracking and email from the start, and gave the checkout a clearer path.
improvement: The team could finally see which channel brought each customer and follow up based on what people actually did, rather than sending the same message to everyone. Numbers started agreeing across tools.
lesson: A store is a system, not a storefront. The value comes from what it connects to, not from the design alone.
