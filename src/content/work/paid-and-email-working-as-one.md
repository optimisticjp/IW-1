---
title: Paid advertising and email, working as one
capabilityTags: ['attract', 'retain', 'connect']
audienceTags: ['ecommerce-brands']
situation: A brand ran paid ads and email as two separate efforts, managed by different people, with no shared view of what a customer had done.
obstacle: Ad spend could not learn from real sales, and email went out on a fixed schedule regardless of whether someone had just bought.
customerExperience: People who had already purchased still received "complete your order" reminders, which felt careless and eroded trust.
change: We connected sales information back to the ad platforms and tied email flows to what customers browsed and bought, so each channel informed the other.
improvement: Ads began spending on people who resembled real buyers, and email stopped contradicting recent purchases. The two channels reinforced each other instead of working blind.
lesson: Channels are only as good as the information they share. Connect the data first, then the campaigns get easier.
