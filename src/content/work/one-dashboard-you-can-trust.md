---
title: One dashboard a team could finally trust
capabilityTags: ['connect']
audienceTags: ['established-teams']
situation: An established team had plenty of tools and plenty of reports, but every department measured differently and no one owned the whole picture.
obstacle: Weekly meetings started with an argument about whose number was right, which meant decisions were delayed or made on the loudest opinion.
customerExperience: Not customer-facing directly, but slow, contested decisions meant slower responses to what customers were doing.
change: We fixed the tracking foundations, agreed one source of truth, and brought the numbers that mattered into a single view the whole team shared.
improvement: The team stopped debating the numbers and started debating the decisions. Reporting took less time to assemble and was trusted enough to act on.
lesson: Numbers disagree when each tool measures differently. Agree one source of truth before building any dashboard on top.
